Plan for three outcomes

Current eastern corridor

Highest current supplied point concern: western Florida Panhandle.

Response: Prioritize wind, rain, coastal access and backup-power readiness.

Track shifts west / east

Strongest effects move with the center and asymmetric bands.

Response: Reorder site priorities each cycle; center lines are not impact boundaries.

Worst credible disruption

Stronger winds combine with flooding, outages and blocked access near landfall.

Response: Plan for loss of multiple services; no invented odds, depths or outage duration.

Read the limits

  • StormGeo points are not verified properties. Wind ranges are maximum likely hourly observations; gusts differ from sustained wind.
  • Probabilities use different points/windows: StormGeo passage; NHC five-day one-minute sustained wind. No averaging.
  • Private rain totals lack an explicit period; coastal-water values lack a datum. No hotel flood depth is established.
  • StormWatch regional window: Fri 6 a.m.–Sun 6 a.m. CDT. Tornado-band window: Fri 4 p.m.–Sat 7 p.m.; mainly near/east of center. Dated briefing.
