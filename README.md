Client weather intelligence and operational readiness report for New Orleans, Pensacola, Gulf Breeze and Navarre.

Published site: https://northstarrecovery.github.io/gulf-disturbance-57-briefing/

## Dated forecast

Weather evidence cutoff: October 6, 2026, 9:50 a.m. EDT. StormGeo Advisory 1 is valid October 6 at 9 a.m. CDT. This report is a fixed snapshot and does not update automatically. Follow current official guidance for decisions.

The report distinguishes private StormGeo scenario guidance, official NHC/NWS/WPC guidance, and supporting model snapshots. Community-level forecasts do not establish a particular property's damage, inundation depth, outage duration or evacuation status.
